Privacy Policy — Mê Văn Học

Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Data we access

Mê Văn Học accesses only what is needed to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account via TikTok's official Content Posting API:

  • TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens, used solely to publish videos on the owner's behalf.
  • Video files and captions supplied by the owner for publishing.

2. Data we do not collect

The app does not collect data about TikTok viewers or other TikTok users, and does not run analytics or tracking on any audience.

3. Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored securely by the automation platform that operates the app. Data is never sold and is never shared with third parties, except as required to call TikTok's official API for publishing.

4. Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept only while the owner keeps the app connected. The owner may revoke access at any time from TikTok's authorized-app settings, after which the app can no longer publish.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
